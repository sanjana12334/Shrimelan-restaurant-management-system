Our Story

Cooked with heart, served with pride.

ShriMelan started with one simple idea — bring restaurant-quality vegetarian food, made the traditional way, to every table in Bhopal. From crisp South Indian dosas to slow-simmered North Indian gravies, sizzling tandoor breads and wok-fresh Chinese, every dish is prepared fresh to order using time-tested recipes and quality ingredients.

No shortcuts, no compromises — just honest, flavourful food and warm hospitality, whether you're dining in, picking up, or ordering home delivery.
